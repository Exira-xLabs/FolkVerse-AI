## Corrections made from actual failures

Chinese `什么是` definitions now reach generic explanation routing while local-name and precise/current-fact guards remain enforced. Prompt v8 avoids words that cause unnecessary conservative refusals and corrects conceptual pitfalls: conservation includes restoration; not all pottery is porous; archaeological depth can be disturbed; first-hand interviews can be later; intangible practices can be visible; fossil formation has several mechanisms; live shadow theatre is continuous. Creative adaptations distinguish documented events from imagined detail. Server-rendered statement references preserve original attribution and classification instead of trusting copied model prose.

The external assessment basis includes [ICOM-CC conservation terminology](https://www.icom-cc.org/en/terminology-for-conservation), [V&A ceramics](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/a-z-of-ceramics), [NPS first-hand/secondary evidence](https://home.nps.gov/teachers/classrooms/primary-and-secondary-sources-in-archeology.htm), [UNESCO shadow puppetry](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/chinese-shadow-puppetry-00421) and [UNESCO living heritage](https://ich.unesco.org/en/what-is-intangible-heritage-00003). Per-case basis and qualifications are in the machine assessment. These evaluation references do not expand the runtime trusted-host allowlist or turn general sections into verified citations.

Human-approved publication is separate; the 448 research drafts are not silently approved. The source inventories/research queue continue broader acquisition. No known unsupported precise-fact publication, invented citation, cross-session access, exposed credential, revoked-evidence reuse or silent/nonterminal delivery remains in the checked release cases. Independent specialist and physical-device review can strengthen later releases; neither is an owner worksheet blocking this closure.
