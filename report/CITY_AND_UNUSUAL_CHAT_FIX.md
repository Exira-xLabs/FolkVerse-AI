# City questions and unusual messages

Basic city questions now search official city information before individual cultural profiles. Examples include “Where is Dalian?”, “Do you know Dalian?”, “do u know dalian”, “大连在哪里？” and “你知道大连吗？”. All 14 Liaoning cities have bilingual administrative-membership facts sourced from the [Liaoning government's administrative table](https://www.ln.gov.cn/web/sqgk/xzqh/index.shtml).

The live lookup fetches the original registered HTTPS page and checks its content hash before permitting a fact. Changed or unavailable pages fail closed. These short bilingual projections are machine-assessed; no human review is claimed. They establish city/province membership, not exhaustive geography, current prices, opening hours or every cultural fact. Questions mentioning an attraction, several cities or attached operational details continue through the existing specific-source lookup and factual-support checks.

The conversation router has message-specific polite wording for unclear messages, unrelated requests, insults and requests to invent sources. Available wording excludes the last two consented replies, and option order is randomized. Generic requests such as “Explain how to repair my car” now pass through domain routing before general explanation generation. No conversation history is retained without consent.
