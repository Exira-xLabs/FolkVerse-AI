Prototype target · not current status

First demo scope: one region, ten reviewed exhibits, thirty rights-cleared artifact records, one branching story, English/Chinese, and one connected visitor loop. Counts are objectives; show actual counts if the team lands short.

Later pilot target: three regions, thirty exhibits, three hundred artifact records. Expansion follows only after the narrow prototype is verified.

Critical handoffs

  • Phase 02’s approved passages unlock the grounded guide; imported records remain drafts until reviewed.
  • Object Lens needs both an eligible catalog and the guide/provider path; no calibrated likely match means no live identity claim.
  • Generated map art is scenery. Selectable regions must use a separate reviewed, data-driven geography layer.
  • At every handoff, mark features as implemented, fixture-driven, untested, or unavailable.
